## Mechanism analysis (REFRAMES QA/admin's "drop, not prime")
QA/admin inferred the stall is the synchronous outgoing-decoder **Drop/eviction** (F-1's `2ead796` half), from `begin_slide_load load_us=64µs` (load was fast). Evidence says otherwise:

1. **`load_us` ≠ prime.** `load_us=64µs` is only the `Mp4Demuxer::open`/cache lookup. The **prime** (`prime_video_decoder`: feed SPS/PPS/IDR + warmup + **block on the first CAPTURE frame**) is a SEPARATE synchronous step, not counted in `load_us`.
2. **`OPENMARQUEE_PRELOAD_MODE=defer`** (confirmed in journal: `preload_mode=defer preload_lead_seconds=1.0s`). Defer short-circuits the preload worker → **no `preload_handoff` lines at all** in the journal → the prime happens **synchronously on the advance/BeginSlide path**.
3. **`e68ecb3`'s own title:** "off-thread V4L2 prime at BeginSlide for cold video paths (**kills 1.5-2.6s render-thread freezes**)" — the exact symptom. 503ms is the warmer/720p-adjacent tail of that same block.
4. **`2ead796` is TRANSITION-scoped** ("evict from-side at end-of-transition"). fireplacesign is **cut-only (transitions=0)** → `2ead796` never fires here → it cannot be this symptom's cause.
5. `evict_other_video_state_exit` shows `v3d_bos=17` **stable** (decoder not fully dropping) and the stall varies (49–507ms) — inconsistent with a fixed drop cost.

**Conclusion (to CONFIRM, not assume):** the ~503ms is the **synchronous first-frame prime at BeginSlide** (defer mode). The fix is the **async-prime half** (`e68ecb3` + its BLOCKER-3 follow-up `445766c`), NOT the transition-eviction half (`2ead796`).
